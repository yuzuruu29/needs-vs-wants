## 2. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
Most budgeting apps are ledgers. Needs vs Wants is a behaviour trainer.

Instead of categorising expenses into dozens of complex buckets at month-end, Needs vs Wants forces a single, immediate binary choice on every purchase:

Is this a NEED, or is this a WANT?

By confronting impulse spending the moment money leaves your wallet, you build permanent mindful spending habits.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✦ KEY FEATURES & HOW IT WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. THE FORCED CHOICE
Every entry requires an honest tap: Green for Need (staples/essentials), Crimson for Want (impulse/indulgence). Watch your real-time Donut Split shift dynamically.

2. PAPER COSTING LEDGER
Built on a tactile, supermarket-premium design inspired by traditional ruled paper ledgers. Zero glassmorphic clutter, zero generic AI design. Responsive animations with reduced-motion support.

3. OPTIONAL DAILY BUDGET
Set a daily spending limit directly on your ledger sheet. If a purchase would cause an overspend, the app pauses you with a mindful confirmation: "Log anyway?"

4. 100% OFFLINE & PRIVATE
Your financial diary belongs to you. All purchase entries, prices, notes, and budgets are stored exclusively in your device's local database. We never upload your diary data to any server.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✦ MEMBERSHIP TIERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FREE TIER — The Core Trainer
• 5 sealed entries per day with carry-forward streak for active days
• Up to 20 lines per ledger sheet
• 30-day rolling history with Need/Want split analytics
• Optional rewarded ad to unlock bonus logs when you need extra entries
• Zero accounts required — start logging immediately

PRO TIER — Unlimited Ledger & Receipt Scanner
• Unlimited daily entries & sheets
• Permanent lifetime history with CSV export & SAF auto-backup
• On-Device ML Kit Receipt Scanner: Scan supermarket and store receipts directly into your ledger. Triaged in seconds with instant Need/Want line sorting (100% on-device OCR — photos are never uploaded)
• History row editing & undo-delete protection

MAX TIER — Financial Advisor & 3-Day Recovery Plans
• Everything in Pro, plus:
• Cited Spending Advisor: Offline economic behavioral coach grounded in referenced study notebooks
• Pre-Seal Want Hold: Mindful 24-hour cooling-off pauses on non-essential impulse items
• 3-Day Compensatory Recovery Plans: Automatically calculates structured recovery budgets following an overspend day

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
✦ PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY

• No account required to use the free core app.
• Diary data, expenses, and receipt photos never leave your device.
• Receipt OCR happens entirely on-device via Google ML Kit.
• Subscriptions and billing are securely managed through Google Play.
```
