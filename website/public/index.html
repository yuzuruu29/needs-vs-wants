The Rule

Every expense is a Need or a Want.

One line, two columns. You decide which side of the page your money lands on — in the aisle, at the counter, in the moment.

Need

Something you must have to live and work. Honest, necessary, unavoidable — the spending you defend without hesitation.

VS

Want

Something you would like to have. Pleasant, optional, often impulsive — the spending that deserves a second look.

Tap an example to seal it — the same one-tap choice the app asks of you.

How It Works

Four rules. No fine print.

The whole method fits on one page of a diary. The daily budget is optional — set it when you want the guardrail.

01

Every expense is a Need or a Want

Each purchase forces a single binary choice. You confront impulse spending in real time, not at month-end.

02

The diary keeps 35 days

Entries older than 35 days are automatically removed. This is a trainer, not an archive.

03

Rows seal themselves

The moment you enter item, cost, and type, the row seals — stamped with the current time and saved instantly.

04

An optional daily budget

Set a limit on Log — the meter there (and Summary Day) shows today's spend, remaining budget, and when you're over. Logging would push you over? The diary asks before you seal — you still decide.

  1. Set it on Log. Open Log, enter your daily limit — say ₱1,500 — and Save budget.
  2. Watch the meter. On Log and Summary Day, see spent vs budget and what's left. Green keeps you on track; red tells you you're over.
  3. Seal past the line? The diary asks “Log anyway?” first. Cancel keeps your draft; Log seals it — your call, every time.
  4. Change or turn off anytime. On Log, tap Change to update the limit, or Turn off to drop the guardrail. The trainer stays.
